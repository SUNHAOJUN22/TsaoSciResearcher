# Definition of done

- English and Chinese negation, clause scope, and contrast cases are regression-tested.
- Evidence maturity and relation to the target claim remain separate.
- Cross-dimension comparisons are blocked; same-dimension units are canonicalized.
- Ready handoff, execution receipt, and accepted state consume exact-bound verified attestations.
- State verification replays from genesis and detects deletion, reordering, duplicates, and tampering.
- Traceability resolves real records and hashes in both directions.
- Capsule validation is streaming and archive-safe.
- Missing external execution or qualified approval remains HOLD.
